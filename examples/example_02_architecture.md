# Example 02 — Building a Dependency Map

**Input:** "I want to launch a startup"

**Typical AI response:** Here are 10 steps to launch a startup...

**OMEGA approach — full dependency chain:**

```
GOAL: Launch startup
│
├── REAL ROOT: What problem exists that people pay to solve?
│   └── Micro-crack: "startup" is a form, not a function
│
├── DEPENDENCY MAP:
│   Market understanding → Product definition → Distribution channel
│        ↓                      ↓                    ↓
│   Who has the pain?    What removes pain?    How do they find it?
│
├── HUMAN FACTOR (Block 5):
│   Most startups fail not from bad product — from wrong distribution
│   Inertia: people don't change behavior without extreme pain
│
└── REALITY CHECK:
    Virtual: "I have a great idea"
    Real: "Someone paid me for this"
```

**Output:** Before building anything — find 3 people who will pay NOW.
That is the only validation that matters.

**OMEGA principle applied:**
Find existing pain → map dependencies → verify against reality → then build.
